# Idea shortlist — November 2026

Research: vidIQ outliers + trending, DIY garden / concrete / pet-upcycling
Shorts from Aug–Nov 2026. "Breakout" = views vs. that channel's normal video.

## What is working right now

| Pattern | Proof (views · breakout) |
|---|---|
| **Plastic bottle → "luxury" planter/vase** | 1.2M in 3 days (Sahil Crafts) · 696K 185x (Satisfy Space) · 1.5M 36x (Alena Frame) |
| **Old tire → luxury garden piece** | Tire sink 3.3M 101x (Buzzqo, *AI-labeled*) · gold marble stepping stones 3.6M · tire table 115x |
| **Junk → house for a cat/dog** | Bottle cat house 1.9M 81x in 1 week · water-jug cat house 585K · barrel cat house 111K |
| **Rescue story hook** | "Stray cats freezing… so I built them a home" · "Rusted trash → cat shelter" 1.8M · boy + dog shelter 8.3M 1997x |
| **Fabric dipped in cement** | Wedding dress → garden art 914K · old jeans planter 14x |
| **Weird household mould** | Bra mould ladybugs 889K · waste cups honeycomb planter 626K 50x |

**Lessons**
- The word **"luxury"** plus a premium finish (gold veins, marble, mosaic) is in most of the top titles.
- Best length: **16–30 s**.
- AI-labeled videos still reach millions (Buzzqo tire sink 3.3M), so the label doesn't hurt reach.
- Title formulas that keep winning:
  - *"You won't believe this was a ___"*
  - *"I turned ___ into a LUXURY ___"*
  - *"___ was freezing… so I built ___"*
