# Idea shortlist — 2026-11-08 (from `references/`)

Source: the 5 Instagram compilations in `references/`, analysed frame by
frame: about 40 projects in total. (An earlier version of this file wrongly
analysed `renders/`.)

## What these videos do

1. **Many projects per video.** 5–10 projects, about **10–12 s each**, 5–8
   shots per project, music only, no voice. A clock icon marks time skips.
2. **Fast per-project structure:**
   ordinary object → one striking process shot (dip, pour, melt, unmould)
   → finish (gold spray, mosaic gems, paint) → **beauty reveal in a
   different, luxurious place**.
3. **The reveal location is upscale.** Items are built in a yard, on a
   driveway or at a workbench, and the last shot shows them in a **luxury
   living room, a villa pool terrace, a restaurant, or at night with lights
   on**. That's where the "wow" comes from.
4. **Payoffs:** night glow (bottle steps, mosaic lamps), fire lit (fire
   pits), water running (mosaic sink), a luxury room reveal (aquarium, gold
   planters), a path or flower bed finished.
5. **Finishes that read as luxury:** gold paint, coloured glass gems or
   bottle mosaic, terrazzo or marble swirl, black and gold.
6. **Recurring object tricks:** clothes dipped in cement (jeans, skirt,
   dress, hat), cardboard moulds cut into letters or shapes, bottles and jars
   set into concrete (they glow at night), melting waste into tiles, big junk
   into furniture (oil drum → chair, tractor tire → aquarium).
7. They use many different people. **Our edge is Luna:** one recognisable
   character across every project.

## What changes for Luna

- **Format A, compilation Short:** "3 Junk Items → Luxury Home" in 30–40 s:
  3 projects × about 11 s. More value per Short means more rewatches. Each
  project is its own mini-plan (hero + 3–4 frames + 3–4 clips).
- **Format B, single project:** keep it, but faster: **12–18 s**, about 5 shots.
- **Two settings per project:** build at a work location, then a **separate
  beauty reveal** in a luxury place. The reveal is its own clip from its own
  hero, so the cut hides the change of place and nothing has to drift.
- **Night is a payoff:** anything with glass, bottles, fire or lights ends
  at dusk with the light on.
