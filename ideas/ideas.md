enjoys it at the end.** None of these copy a project from the example
channels (mop feeder, flamingo, gloves, dungarees, umbrella sink, tire sink,
terrazzo table, dog topiary, paw-print path are all taken).

Keep a mix of ~25 s simple ones and ~50 s story ones (a small problem at the
start — *"the puppy had nowhere to shelter"* — then the build, then the
payoff). The story format had the highest views per day in the examples.

## Rule for picking ideas

The junk's shape must stay recognisable **and have a job** in the finished
piece (Make & Reborn: the gloves become hands that *hold* the bowl). Design
the finished piece first; build every step image from it.
