# 012 — Tire Duck Pond

**Title:** These Ducklings Had Nowhere to Swim… So I Used an Old Tire 🦆
**Built:** result first (the beautiful piece), then the object whose shape makes it.
**Status:** frames done and reviewed (9/9) · clips: 0/5 · edited: no

Written to [the realism playbook](../../brand/realism-playbook.md). Make it by hand with [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a small round garden pond about 75 cm across, set into the lawn beside the stone path: its rim is covered in rounded grey and tan river stones set in mortar, a flat stone ramp leads into it, clear water with three green water-lily pads.
- **Junk:** an old black car tire about 65 cm wide, dusty with worn tread.
- **The junk's job:** the tire is the pond's round wall and rim; its circle is the pond's outline, and its rubber holds the stones.
- **Shape test:** tire 65 cm ring + 5 cm of stones on the rim → a 75 cm round pond. ✔ The circle is the tire's own.
- **Payoff:** three fluffy yellow ducklings hop off the stone ramp into the water and paddle.

**Built in place** on Luna's stone path; same camera and spot in every shot.
