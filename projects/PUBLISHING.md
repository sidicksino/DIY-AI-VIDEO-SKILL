# Publishing schedule: batch 1 (5 Shorts)

Upload all 5 the same day and **schedule** them one per day, all at the same
time. **17:00 your local time** is a good default. Daily posting teaches the
algorithm that the channel is active. Each Short also gets its own test
audience, instead of 5 Shorts competing with each other in the same hour.

| Day | Project | Title | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 010 kitten igloo | This Kitten Was Freezing… So I Built Her a Stone Igloo 🥺🐱 | Ready now; the strongest story and payoff, so it leads |
| 2 | 014 terrazzo cat bed | I Poured Concrete Between Two Bowls… Now My Cat Won't Leave It 🐱 | Two satisfying reveals (unmould, sanding) |
| 3 | 011 leaf bird bath | I Pressed Concrete Over a Giant Leaf… The Birds Love It 🍃🐦 | Leaf-peel reveal; a different animal |
| 4 | 012 tire duck pond | These Ducklings Had Nowhere to Swim… So I Used an Old Tire 🦆 | Story video with a very cute payoff |
| 5 | 013 hedgehog house | A Hedgehog Was Hiding in the Cold… So I Built Him a Stone House 🦔 | Story video to close the week |

Each project's `post.md` has the title, description, tags and TikTok
caption, ready to paste.

## Every upload

1. **YouTube Studio → Create → Upload videos →** `output/final.mp4`.
2. Paste the **title**, **description** and **tags** from `post.md`.
3. **Altered or synthetic content: Yes.**
4. **Visibility → Schedule →** the day and time above.
5. **TikTok:** post the same file with the TikTok caption, and turn on
   *AI-generated content*.

## After day 3

Check Studio → Analytics. Note which Short has the highest **"viewed vs.
swiped away"** rate and the most average view duration. The next batch from
`/luna-ideas` should lean toward that format (story vs. satisfying reveal,
cat vs. birds).
