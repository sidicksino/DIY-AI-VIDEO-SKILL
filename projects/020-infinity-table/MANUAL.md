**Video:** I Made a Luxury Infinity Table From Old Boxes and Concrete ✨

Claude makes and reviews the 11 frames first. When Claude says they're approved, make these 6 clips.

**Settings for every clip:** Video → **Frames** · **Veo 3.1 Fast** (use **Veo 3.1 Quality** for **C6**) · **9:16** · **x1** · 8 s · **resolution 1080p, set BEFORE you generate** (click "720p" in the composer and pick 1080p; 4K if your plan offers it). Download at that same size.
Pick **Start** and **End** from this project's `frames/*.png`. **C6: Start frame only, leave End empty.** Save as **`clips/raw/<clip>.mp4`**, then tell Claude "clips ready".

**These prompts are written second by second so the whole 8 s is usable.** Use the Check line under each prompt: if the take matches at 2 s, 5 s and 8 s, it's a keeper.
