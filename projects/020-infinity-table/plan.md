## Concept

- **Finished piece:** a coffee table: an infinity-shaped concrete base, a solid band 9 cm wide forming a figure-eight, 100 cm long, 45 cm tall and 35 cm deep, standing on its edge, glossy deep emerald green with a thin bright gold line along the front inner edge of each round loop, under a round clear glass top 90 cm across.
- **Junk:** old brown corrugated moving boxes, flattened and cut into long strips 35 cm wide, scuffed and creased.
- **The junk's job:** the cardboard *is* the mould. Its bent outline becomes the table's outline; its strip width (35 cm) becomes the table's depth.
- **Shape test:** outer strip bent into a figure-eight 100 × 45 cm + an inner wall of two teardrop loops 9 cm inside it → a 9 cm concrete channel → stood on its edge: a 100 cm long, 45 cm tall, 35 cm deep infinity base. Glass 90 cm rests on the two loop tops. ✔
- **Placement:** centred in front of the cream boucle sofa on a soft cream rug, the way a coffee table sits. Dressed with white hydrangeas, two books and a brass bowl on the glass. Framed as the subject, sofa and windows behind. ✔
- **Ending:** `use` — Luna sets the vase on the glass while the camera glides along the table and rises to the whole room (3–4 s of travel).
- **Format:** steps (6 full clips, build clips at 1.5×, ≈ 33 s).
- **Opening action:** frame 0, Luna is already bending the cardboard strip around the left inner loop; tape contact by second 7.
- **Reason:** none written; the hook is the question "what is she making from old boxes?". The use beat closes it.
