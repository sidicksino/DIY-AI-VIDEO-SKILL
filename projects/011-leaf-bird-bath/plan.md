# 011 — Leaf Bird Bath

**Title:** I Pressed Concrete Over a Giant Leaf… The Birds Love It 🍃🐦
**Built:** result first (the beautiful piece), then the object whose shape makes it.
**Status:** frames done and reviewed (8/8) · clips: 0/5 · edited: no

Written to [the realism playbook](../../brand/realism-playbook.md). Make it by hand with [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a leaf-shaped concrete bird bath about 60 cm long and 45 cm wide: a shallow bowl in the exact outline of a giant rhubarb leaf, with deep vein lines, painted mossy green with soft gold on the veins, resting on a short round grey stone pedestal about 40 cm tall, filled with clear water.
- **Junk:** one giant rhubarb leaf from the garden, about 60 × 45 cm, plus a mound of damp sand to shape it on.
- **The junk's job:** the leaf is the mould: laid vein-side up over a sand mound, its outline becomes the bowl's outline and its veins are printed into the concrete. Peeling the leaf off shows where the shape came from.
- **Shape test:** leaf 60 × 45 cm over a sand dome + 3 cm concrete → a 60 × 45 cm leaf-shaped bowl. ✔ The outline is the leaf's own.
- **Payoff:** a robin lands on the rim and splashes in the water.

**Built in place** on Luna's stone path; same camera and spot in every shot.
