## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a small white stone igloo cat shelter, a round dome
  about 55 cm across and 33 cm tall. It has a rough concrete shell scored
  into igloo blocks, a soft white limewash finish with grey concrete
  texture showing through, and an arched 22 cm doorway with a short
  entrance lip. A folded cream knit blanket sits inside.
- **Junk:** an old blue 55 cm exercise ball, scuffed and dusty. It is
  **half-inflated**, so it slumps on the board into a low round dome about
  50 cm across and 30 cm tall.
- **The junk's job:** the ball is the mould. Concrete goes over its dome,
  and once the concrete cures, Luna lets the air out and pulls the
  crumpled ball out through the doorway. That shows the viewer exactly
  where the round shape came from.
- **Shape test:** a slumped ball, 50 × 30 cm dome, + 3 cm of concrete →
  a 55 × 33 cm dome. ✔ That's the igloo's outline and size.
- **Payoff:** the same small ginger kitten from the rain shot walks into
  the igloo and curls up on the blanket.

**Built in place,** on a plywood board on the stone path beside the
hydrangeas. Every shot uses the same camera and spot.
