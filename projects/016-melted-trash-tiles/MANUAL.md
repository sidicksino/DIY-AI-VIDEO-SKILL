# 016 — make the clips in Flow by hand

**Video:** I Melted 3 Kinds of Trash Into Luxury Tiles 🔥

Claude makes and reviews all 21 frames first. When Claude says the frames are approved, make these 15 clips.

**Settings for every clip:** Video → **Frames** · **Veo 3.1 Fast** (use **Veo 3.1 Quality** for the REVEAL/PAYOFF clip) · **9:16** · **x1** · 8 s · **resolution 1080p, set BEFORE you generate** (click "720p" in the composer and pick 1080p; 4K if your plan offers it). Download at that same size. Switch off the Flow Connector extension first.
Pick **Start** (and **End** where given) from this project's clean `frames/*.png`. Watch each clip once; regenerate once if clearly broken.
Save as **`clips/raw/<clip>.mp4`** (e.g. `clips/raw/P1-C1.mp4`), then tell Claude "clips ready". Doing one project (5 clips) at a time is fine.

### P1-C1 — melt it
**Start:** `P1-S1` · **End:** `P1-S2` · save as **`clips/raw/P1-C1.mp4`**
```
Vertical smartphone video on a tripod, fixed framing, real-time speed, one continuous shot. Blue gas flames lick the wide black steel wok as the thousands of colourful plastic bottle caps inside soften and melt into thick molten white plastic swirled with bright speckles of red, blue, yellow, green and orange while Luna stirs it with a long steel paddle. The wok, burner and bench stay exactly in place. Natural motion blur, true-to-life colors, no color grading, warm workshop light. SFX: roaring gas flame, bubbling, the paddle scraping steel. Ambient: workshop hum. No music, no voices, no on-screen text.
```
