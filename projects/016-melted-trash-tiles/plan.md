# 016 — I melted 3 kinds of trash into luxury tiles (compilation)

**Title:** I Melted 3 Kinds of Trash Into Luxury Tiles 🔥
**Format:** A, a 3-project compilation, after `references/-4620642025400158218.mp4` (its first three projects: melted waste → tile moulds → steam → luxury path). Same format, our own wastes, tiles, places and endings.
**Tags:** [cat:melt-tiles · melt-workshop→luxury-bathroom/villa-lawn/luxury-kitchen · payoff:luxury-room-reveal · fmt:A · end:match-cut/lights-on/use]
**Status:** frames done and reviewed (21/21) · clips: 15/15 · edited: final ready (39 s)

Written to [the realism playbook](../../brand/realism-playbook.md). Clips are made by hand with [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## Concept

Each project is about 10 s, with the same five beats as the reference: **melt in a wok over fire → pour into a steel mould → cold water, steam burst → lift the glossy tile → reveal it laid in a luxury place**. Luna wears heat gloves and safety glasses for the hot work.

| | Waste | Tile (shape = the mould ✔) | Reveal place (placement check) | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | a tall heap of thousands of colourful plastic bottle caps | a square 40 × 40 cm, 2 cm thick tile of glossy white recycled plastic speckled with chips of red, blue, yellow, green and orange, like terrazzo | `luxury-bathroom`: covers the whole bathroom floor around a freestanding tub (job: floor; backdrop: tub and windows; dressing: towels, plant) | `match-cut` |
| P2 | a tall heap of crushed aluminium drink cans | a round stepping stone 40 cm wide and 4 cm thick of polished silver aluminium with a mirror shine | `villa-lawn`: a curving path across a villa lawn to a sunken fire-pit lounge, with path lights (job: path; backdrop: fire pit and villa) | `lights-on` |
| P3 | a tall heap of empty green plastic bottles | a long rectangular 10 × 30 cm tile of glossy jade-green recycled plastic with soft white marble-like swirls | `luxury-kitchen`: the full splashback behind a marble kitchen island (job: wall; dressing: brass tap, pendants, a lemon bowl) | `use` |

**Variety inside the video:** 3 wastes, 3 colours, 3 shapes (square, round, long), 3 places, 3 ending styles. The previous video (015) ended without Luna, so Luna may appear in the final shot (P3).

**Original vs the reference:** they used amber bottles, tyres and mixed bottles for an amber path, a black path and a turquoise hexagon patio. Ours: bottle caps → terrazzo floor, aluminium cans → silver stepping stones, green bottles → jade splashback.

## Fixed descriptions

- **Luna:** Luna, a woman in her mid-twenties with light-brown skin, long curly dark-brown hair with caramel highlights pulled into a high ponytail, freckles across her nose and cheeks, brown eyes, small gold hoop earrings, a bright friendly smile and a fit, athletic figure. She wears a fitted sage-green ribbed cropped tank top with a small white leaf logo on the chest, high-waisted light-blue denim jeans, a brown leather tool belt and tan leather work boots. Grey work gloves when handling concrete.
- **Workshop (build place for all three):** a big recycling workshop with a heavy steel workbench, a gas burner stand holding a wide black steel wok, square and round steel tile moulds and long steel ladles on the bench, tall piles of the waste material behind, warm work lights and a big open roller door
- **Safety gear:** She wears heavy brown leather heat gloves and clear safety glasses for the hot work.
