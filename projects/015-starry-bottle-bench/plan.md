## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a long white concrete garden bench, 140 cm long, 45 cm
  high and 40 cm deep, with crisp square edges and a smooth satin finish. Its
  front face is set with 3 neat rows of 14 glass bottle bottoms (42 in all)
  in amber, bottle green, clear and cobalt blue. At night, warm light inside
  the bench makes every bottle bottom glow like a star.
- **Junk:** 42 empty glass wine and beer bottles (amber, green, clear,
  cobalt blue), plus a long open-top plywood box mould.
- **The junk's job:** the bottles lie horizontally inside the mould, bottoms
  pressed against the front board. When the board comes off, their round
  bottoms *are* the stars in the bench face, and they carry the light.
- **Shape test:** inner mould 140 × 45 × 40 cm → bench 140 × 45 × 40 cm.
  Bottles about 30 cm long fit within the 40 cm depth. ✔ The bench is the
  mould's own shape, and the circles are the bottles' own bottoms.
- **Placement:** on the villa's stone garden terrace, backed by a tall clipped
  hedge between two uplit olive trees, with a rug, a low table, two glasses of
  wine and a lantern in front: a real evening seating spot.
- **Ending:** `pull-back`. Luna switches it on (C5); the last shot starts close
  on the glowing bottles and pulls back to show the whole lit terrace, with
  nobody in it.

**Build place:** `driveway` · **Reveal place:** `villa-lawn` at dusk
(`brand/locations.md`).
