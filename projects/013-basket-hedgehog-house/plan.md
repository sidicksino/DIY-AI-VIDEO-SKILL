# 013 — Basket Hedgehog House

**Title:** A Hedgehog Was Hiding in the Cold… So I Built Him a Stone House 🦔
**Built:** result first (the beautiful piece), then the object whose shape makes it.
**Status:** frames done and reviewed (9/9) · clips: 5/5 · edited: final ready

Written to [the realism playbook](../../brand/realism-playbook.md). Make it by hand with [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a round stone hedgehog house, a low dome about 40 cm across and 25 cm tall, its pale grey cement shell showing the woven pattern of a wicker basket, an arched 12 cm doorway, a cushion of green moss on top and dry autumn leaves at the entrance.
- **Junk:** an old round wicker laundry basket about 38 cm across and 23 cm deep, turned upside down.
- **The junk's job:** the basket is the mould and the frame: coated in cement slurry it keeps its dome shape, and its weave shows through as texture.
- **Shape test:** upturned basket 38 × 23 cm + 1 cm cement → a 40 × 25 cm woven dome. ✔ The dome and the weave are the basket's own.
- **Payoff:** the same hedgehog from the cold opening shot shuffles into the doorway.

**Built in place** on Luna's stone path; same camera and spot in every shot.
