## Concept

- **Finished piece:** a round kintsugi stone coffee table: an 85 cm diameter, 45 cm tall circular table with a solid 4 cm thick dark charcoal slate mosaic tabletop edged in a 5 cm brushed satin brass rim, featuring tight geometric stone fragments separated by narrow 1 cm joints inlaid with radiant metallic gold leaf in a kintsugi pattern, supported by a matching circular brushed brass double-ring architectural base.
