# 014 — Terrazzo Cat Bed

**Title:** I Poured Concrete Between Two Bowls… Now My Cat Won't Leave It 🐱
**Built:** result first (the beautiful piece), then the object whose shape makes it.
**Status:** frames done and reviewed (7/7) · clips: 0/4 · edited: no

Written to [the realism playbook](../../brand/realism-playbook.md). Make it by hand with [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## Concept (result first)

- **Finished piece:** a round terrazzo cat bed, a wide shallow bowl about 55 cm across and 15 cm tall, polished smooth white concrete speckled with chips of terracotta, sage green and black stone, with a round cream knit cushion inside.
- **Junk:** two old plastic washing-up basins: a big blue one about 55 cm across and a smaller grey one about 47 cm across.
- **The junk's job:** the two nested basins are the mould: the big one shapes the outside, the small one pressed inside makes the hollow. Pulling the basin off, then sanding, reveals the terrazzo.
- **Shape test:** 55 cm basin outside + 47 cm basin inside → a 55 × 15 cm bowl with 4 cm walls. ✔ The bowl is the basins' own shape.
- **Payoff:** a fluffy grey tabby cat steps into the bed, kneads the cushion and curls up.

**Built in place** on Luna's stone path; same camera and spot in every shot.
