# 018 — make the clips in Flow by hand

**Video:** I Turned an Old Bike Tyre Into a Glowing Car Display 🚗

Claude makes and reviews the 16 frames first. When Claude says they're approved, make these 10 clips.

**Settings for every clip:** Video → **Frames** · **Veo 3.1 Fast** (use **Veo 3.1 Quality** for **C10**) · **9:16** · **x1** · 8 s · **resolution 1080p, set BEFORE you generate** (click "720p" in the composer and pick 1080p; 4K if your plan offers it). Download at that same size.
Pick **Start** and **End** from this project's `frames/*.png`. **C2, C7 and C9 are close-ups: Start frame only, leave End empty.** Save as **`clips/raw/<clip>.mp4`**, then tell Claude "clips ready".
