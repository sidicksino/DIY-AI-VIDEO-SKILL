# Keywords, titles and hashtags — research 2026-11-14

Source: vidIQ keyword research (YouTube search volume + competition), vidIQ
breakout Shorts (videos far above their channel's average), and vidIQ
Instagram/TikTok outliers, all for junk-to-luxury / transformation content.
Re-run this research every ~2 months; search demand moves fast.

## What actually makes a Short go viral (in order of weight)

1. **First 1–2 seconds + retention.** Every breakout in this niche opens in
   action or on a problem (someone already sawing, pouring or tearing out; a
   ruined room; a small conflict). None opens on the finished piece: see
   `brand/growth-playbook.md`. Tags barely move Shorts; the title and the
   opening do.
2. **Title = a transformation promise.** The winners all follow a few
   patterns (below). Search words in the title also make the video findable.
3. **Fast time-lapse transformation of a whole space** is the hottest format
   right now on all three platforms (bunker → bedroom 2–5M, concrete pipe →
   tiny home 15M, abandoned boat → cabin 9M, forgotten room 18M).
4. **Keywords/tags/hashtags** only tell the platform *what* the video is and
   whom to test it on. Use them correctly (below), but they are the last 10%.

## Title patterns that broke out (copy the pattern, never the title)

| Pattern | Real breakout (views, × channel average) |
|---|---|
| **I Turned [junk] Into [luxury result] [emoji]** | "I Turned Concrete Into a Stunning Teddy Bear Coffee Table 🧸✨" 613K, 16× |
| **From [junk] to [luxury result]** | "From Old Tires to a Luxury Clover Coffee Table" 446K |
| **[Junk] did NOT deserve to be thrown away 😳** | "This $20 Goodwill Table Did NOT Deserve to Be Thrown Away 😳" 167K, 27× |
| **Abandoned / forgotten [place] → [dream result]** | "A forgotten room, given a fresh new beginning ✨" 18M, 730× |
| **[Number] junk [things] into a high-end [result]?** | "3 junk barstools into a high end wine bar?" 875K |
| **Look what was underneath / hiding under** | "…Look What Was Underneath 😳" 3.4M, 22× |
| **Turning [junk] into [result] for [someone]** (story) | "Turning A Plastic Shelter Into A Cozy Home…" 3.5M IG |
| **[Problem]. [Answer].** (two short sentences, no hashtags) | "He Splashed Her Book. She Built a Pool." 131M · "The Stairs Gave Up. She Didn't." 2.1M · "These Empty Bottles Got a Glowing Retirement" 1.2M |

Rules: no hashtags inside the title (they go in the description); alternate
the "I Turned…" pattern with the two-sentence story pattern; under ~60 characters; the junk word first (it's the curiosity), the
luxury result second; one emoji at the end (😳 ✨ 💎 🏠); words that recur in
breakouts: *turned, junk, old, abandoned, forgotten, luxury, dream, high-end,
transformation, thrown away*.

## Hashtags

- **YouTube** (description end, max 3–5): `#upcycling #transformation #diyhome #shorts` + one topic tag (`#bedroommakeover`, `#furnitureflip`, `#gardenmakeover`).
- **Instagram** (3–5, specific beats generic): `#upcycling #diytransformation #beforeandafter #homemakeover` + one topic tag (`#furnitureflip`, `#dreamroom`, `#bedroommakeover`, `#gardenmakeover`).
- **TikTok** (3–5): `#diytransformation #upcycling #beforeandafter #homedecor` + one topic tag (`#dreamroom`, `#furnitureflip`, `#roommakeover`, `#gardentok`).

Breakout captions on IG/TikTok used exactly these: #DIYTransformation,
#BeforeAndAfter, #HomeMakeover, #dreamroom, #furnitureflip, #diytransformation.
Skip #fyp / #viral: no measurable lift.

## Description (YouTube) — first line matters most

First line = the title's promise in other words, with 1–2 search keywords:
"A rusty old bunk bed → a dream kids' room. Full room transformation, start to finish 🏠"
Then the channel footer, then hashtags.
